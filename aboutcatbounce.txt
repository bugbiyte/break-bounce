A tiny desktop app that periodically hijacks the screen with bouncing creatures until the user takes a break.


Core idea:
* Every 45–60 minutes, the app appears on top of everything.
* Cats/goblins/skulls/office gremlins bounce around the screen 
* User cannot dismiss immediately.
* It gives a silly command:“Stand up, mortal. Touch your toes for 30 seconds.”
* After the timer ends, a button appears: “I have appeased the goblin.”
