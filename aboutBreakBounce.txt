A web app that sounds an alarm and pulls you into a "Break Bounce" session with bouncing creatures that prompts challenges driven by a goblin


Core idea:
* User sets timer for golbin creature to remind them for a 'break bounce' sessions
* Cats/goblins/skulls/office gremlins bounce around the screen 
* User cannot dismiss immediately.
* It gives a silly command:“Stand up, mortal. Touch your toes for 30 seconds.”
* After the timer ends, a button appears: “I have appeased the goblin.”

BONUS: If user completes 5 Break Bounce Sessions, the goblin will grant you peace and reveal their face.
